# Model Card: Model E – Climate Risk Scorer

- **Objective**: Evaluates drought, heat stress, excess rainfall, and dry-spell risk windows.
- **Inputs**: Max temperature, seasonal rainfall, crop thermal/moisture thresholds.
- **Outputs**: Composite risk score (0-100), risk level (Low, Moderate, High), advisory note.
- **Fallback**: Rule-based drought/heat stress score.
